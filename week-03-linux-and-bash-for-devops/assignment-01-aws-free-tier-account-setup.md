---

#### Question 2 — What is AWS Free Tier, and how long does it last?

AWS Free Tier allows new customers to explore eligible AWS services within specified limits without paying standard usage charges. Under the current Free Plan, it lasts for up to six months or until the available Free Tier credits are used, whichever happens first. Some AWS services also provide Always Free offers with their own monthly limits.

---

#### Question 3 — Name three AWS Free Tier services and their free usage limits.

Three AWS Free Tier services are AWS Lambda, Amazon DynamoDB, and Amazon SQS. Lambda includes 1 million requests and 400,000 GB-seconds of compute time each month. DynamoDB includes 25 GB of storage, 25 read capacity units, and 25 write capacity units. Amazon SQS includes 1 million requests each month.
